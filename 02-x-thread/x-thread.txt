1/8 Redbelly Network has named Christian Westerlind Wigstrom as its next CEO. The appointment was announced on 16 Sept 2026 and he starts in November.

Who is he, and what could his track record mean for Tokeniser? A short, sourced thread.

2/8 Career: co-founder of payments company Monoova, and its CEO for almost 10 years until 2026.

Industry roles: board of FinTech Australia, and Alternate Director at the Australian Payments Network (AusPayNet).

https://www.bankingday.com/redbelly-announces-paytech-co-founder-christian-westerlind-wigstrom-as-ceo-to-push-into-tokenisation

---

3/8 Monoova by the end of his tenure, per Redbelly:
- $200bn processed
- transactions with more than 10% of Australians
- Australia's largest PayTo processor
- almost a quarter of the country's PayIDs issued

Clients included Wise and Carsales.com.au.

https://redbelly.network/blog/redbelly-new-CEO-is-a-payments-pioneer-betting-on-regulated-tokenisation

---

4/8 His specialism is real-time payments plus automation. In his words, Monoova specialised "in real-time payments and payments automation for businesses", with APIs letting companies "tailor how they receive, manage and pay funds".

https://www.monoova.com/blog/who-are-monova-and-what-do-we-do-a-chat-with-christian-westerlind-wigstrom

---

5/8 Before fintech: a PhD at Oxford, work as an economic advisor and speechwriter in the UK Parliament, then three years as Chief of Staff and Markets at an agricultural startup in Zimbabwe.

https://www.thebusinessconversation.com.au/contributor/christian-westerlind-wigstrom
